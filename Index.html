About Me

Hi, I'm Md. Najmus Shakib — an undergraduate Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET).

I am driven by a passion for converting raw circuit logic and mathematical equations into responsive, intelligent physical devices. My primary work focuses on Embedded Systems, Internet of Things (IoT), and Signal Processing, engineering reliable prototypes from low-level C firmware to interactive software dashboards.

Core Pillars

Embedded Systems

Custom firmware architecture and sensor design using ESP32 & Arduino microcontrollers.

IoT & Telemetry

Wireless data sync (BLE, MQTT, Wi-Fi) connecting hardware directly to cloud services.

Applied Data & ML

Implementing mathematical prediction models directly onto embedded hardware.

Research Interests

  • Machine Learning
  • Signal Processing
  • Communication Systems
  • Biomedical Engineering

Engineering Goal

To continuously innovate across hardware and software domain boundaries, creating dependable real-world systems for industrial and environmental applications.
